Dear Madame,

You have three letters waiting.

Three cities, three gentlemen who know them by heart. As you walk they'll murmur in your ear — the history, the gossip, where to buy the good cheese. They need no internet at all; they are, after all, rather old-fashioned.

Tap a gentleman to read him first:

  • Tap anything on his list and he'll tell you its story.
  • Near me sorts it all by distance from your feet.
  • Pass something good and he may interrupt. Politely.
  • Airplane mode is fine — maps, letters, and all.
